# Clip.md Privacy Policy

_Last updated: September 27, 2026_

Clip.md collects no data.

- Clip.md reads the clipboard only right after you copy in an app you've chosen to watch. It rewrites the plain-text copy as Markdown on your Mac, then stops.
- Clipboard contents are never stored, logged, or sent anywhere. Clip.md makes no network connections.
- The only thing Clip.md saves is its settings (whether it's on and which apps it watches), kept in its sandboxed preferences on your Mac.
- There are no analytics, no crash reporting, no accounts, and no third-party SDKs.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/NorthIsUp/clip.md/issues.
