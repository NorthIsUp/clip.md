This is a scoring guide for the county fair pie contest. a winner needs ≥12 points and at least one “blue ribbon” vote.

Crust & Texture

1 point

4 Points [Blue Ribbon]

  • No soggy bottom
  • Flaky layers and even browning across the whole pie
  • Showed excellent lattice technique as well as a neat crimped edge
  • Tried several decorations

3 Points [Red Ribbon]

  • Mostly crisp crust
  • Slightly uneven browning

1 Point [Participant]

  • Crust fell apart when sliced
